# Venture Star — Mood Board Options

## 1. Horizon Signal

- Palette: Deep Space `#17324A`, Survey Teal `#2DB6A3`, Beacon Coral `#F26B52`, Solar Amber `#F5A623`, Starlight `#F4F1E8`, Mist Blue `#91C9E8`.
- Shape language: symmetrical spacecraft, clipped corners, broad filled planes, `1.5`-unit navy outlines.
- Texture: flat colour with restrained two-tone gradients reserved for shields and discoveries.
- Mood: optimistic, capable, and immediately readable—a modern vector evolution of the reference image's compact spacecraft silhouettes.

## 2. Solar Cartography

- Palette: Chart Navy `#26384A`, Sun Gold `#F6C85F`, Expedition Coral `#EF6A5B`, Habitat Teal `#3AAFA9`, Paper White `#ECF4EF`, Dusk Violet `#6D6A91`.
- Shape language: rounded orbital arcs, friendly capsules, circles and softened trapezoids, `1.25`-unit outlines.
- Texture: flat poster-like planes with occasional orbit lines and warm halos.
- Mood: the warmest and most welcoming option, emphasizing hopeful settlement and discovery over combat.

## 3. Deep Survey

- Palette: Instrument Navy `#16324F`, Scanner Teal `#39B8B2`, Signal Amber `#F3A83B`, Alert Coral `#EF6753`, Ice White `#E9F4F2`, Sensor Blue `#6CC5D9`.
- Shape language: fine-line instruments, radar circles, directional wedges, small solid signal markers, `1.25`-unit strokes.
- Texture: crisp monoline diagrams layered with sparse filled silhouettes and dashed range rings.
- Mood: analytical and exploratory, making sensors, routes, danger contours, and readable AI intent the visual signature.
