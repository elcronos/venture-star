## Cohesion verdict

Set cohesion: 96%. The shared 104-unit disc, ten-unit navy rim, upper-left gradient lighting, palette-only fills, and controlled internal marks unify the family. Terrain direction, plane geometry, and colour distribution keep every planet distinct in grayscale-friendly silhouette and small-scale composition. All assets are pure vector, use `viewBox="0 0 256 256"`, preserve transparent corners, and intentionally omit ownership treatment for composition with the separate A07 patterns.
