# A16 HUD Icons — Production Review

Status: **PASS** — 49/49 accepted at 90%+.

## Scope and method

Reviewed the complete A16 family against `style-guide.md` and the Game Asset Forge critique rubric. Every source was rendered on Night Field (`#091426`) in two alphabetical 7×7 contact sheets:

- 128 px per icon for construction, balance, palette, and cohesion.
- 24 px per icon for production-size silhouette and detail survival.

Iteration 1 established the shared cream/deep-navy construction and semantic accent colours. Iteration 2 removed non-standard 2-unit inner strokes from `cancel`, `danger`, and `unknown`; all sources now use only the required 1.5 outer and 1.0 inner strokes.

## Cohesion verdict

The family is cohesive: filled geometric silhouettes, deep-navy rims, rounded joins, sparse one-unit instrumentation, and one semantic accent per concept. Similar concepts remain distinguishable at 24 px: `close` vs `cancel`, `import` vs `export`, `ore` vs `metal` vs `crystal`, `effects` vs `discovery`, and `shield` vs `armour` vs `hull`.

The lowest target-size scores are 92–93% for stale-intel, defence, influence, route, unknown, and wrapped-route. Each still clears the acceptance threshold; the small secondary marks add meaning without collapsing the primary silhouette.

## Validation evidence

- Exact inventory: 49 expected filenames and 49 SVG files.
- XML: all 49 pass `xmllint --noout`.
- Geometry: all 49 use exactly `viewBox="0 0 32 32"`.
- Transparency: no background rectangles; all render with alpha.
- Typography/raster safety: no `<text>`, `<image>`, script, filter, or external references.
- Palette: only documented Horizon Signal hex values.
- Stroke hierarchy: only `1.5` and `1` stroke widths.
- Bounds: key geometry remains within the two-unit inset.
